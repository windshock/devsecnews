Processing:

- afade in 1s + afade out 1s
- libmp3lame qscale 4   (~140 kbps)

To swap: replace public/audio/bgm.mp3 keeping the filename — Main.jsx picks it
up automatically. For a fully silent banner remove the <Audio> for bgm in
src/compositions/Main.jsx.
